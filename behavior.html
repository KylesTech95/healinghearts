Monitor My Behavior

A positive emotional attitude will serve you well in your personal and professional life. Do your best to identify a goal you want to achieve that can potentially brighten your attitude towards life. This goal should be related to seeking opportunities, believing everything will work out well and looking at the positive side. Monitoring your progress towards the goal can help develop a more positive attitude. Keep track of your behaviors to look back on, reassess your goal if needed, and set new goals to tackle.

Example:
My healthy Behavioral change: I want to stop blaming my situation on other people.
My goal: To take more responsibility for my present life and future life.
